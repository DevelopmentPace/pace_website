About PACE

35 years of compassionate care,
exceptional education

Founded in 1989, PACE has grown from a small school into a comprehensive organization serving individuals with autism at every stage of life.

Mission & Vision

A world where every ability leads to a fulfilled life

Our mission is to enhance the lives of people impacted by autism through innovation, exceptional education, and compassionate care.

Guided by "Connect, Support, Educate," PACE provides individualized attention, specialized teaching, and compassionate care that promotes independence, lifelong learning, and community integration.

We serve individuals and families across Santa Clara County through six core programs spanning preschool through adulthood.

🤝

Connect

Building bridges between families, educators, therapists, and community.

Support

Providing individualized care designed around each person's unique needs.

Educate

Creating exceptional learning experiences that open doors to independence.

Our Values

What guides everything we do

❤️

Compassion

Every interaction is guided by genuine empathy and respect for the dignity of each individual and family.

🔬

Innovation

We apply the latest research in autism education and behavioral science, continually improving our methods.

🌟

Excellence

WASC accreditation and a 4-star Charity Navigator rating reflect our commitment to the highest standards.

🤲

Inclusion

Every individual belongs. We design programs that integrate people with autism into their wider community.

🌱

Growth

We believe in the limitless potential of every person we serve, and design programs to unlock it.

🔗

Partnership

Families are central partners in every plan. We work with — not just for — the people in our care.

Our Story

35 years of milestones

1989

PACE Founded

Pacific Autism Center for Education opens its doors in Santa Clara, serving a small group of students with autism.

Late 1990s

Residential Expansion

PACE grows to include residential services, providing supported community living for adults with autism.

2000s

ABA Services Launch

Faces ABA Behavioral Services launches, extending PACE's reach into evidence-based therapy.

2010s

Porchlight & Preschool

Sunny Days Preschool opens for ages 2½–6. Porchlight online education series launches nationwide.

2024

35th Anniversary

PACE celebrates 35 years of impact, earning Platinum Candid transparency and a 4-star Charity Navigator rating.

Early Childhood

Sunny Days Preschool

An inclusive preschool in San Jose serving children with and without developmental disabilities, ages 2½–6. Speech, OT, and special education specialists work side-by-side in integrated classrooms.

K–12 & Transition

PACE School

A WASC-accredited non-public school providing specialized instruction for students ages 6–22 with autism. Small class sizes, individualized education plans, and vocational transition programming.

Ages 6–22WASC accreditedPruneridge & San Aleso campusesVocational transition

ABA Behavioral Services

Faces ABA Behavioral Services

Evidence-based Applied Behavior Analysis therapy for children in home, clinic, and community settings. Faces BCBAs use a trauma-informed, naturalistic approach focused on meaningful outcomes for the whole family.
